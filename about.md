<p>
I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics at <strong>Purdue University</strong>, where I’m advised by <strong>Dr. Peijun Li</strong> and <strong>Dr. Isaac Harris</strong>. My research interests broadly lie in the analysis of <strong>partial differential equations</strong> and <strong>inverse scattering problems</strong>.
</p>

<p>
Beyond mathematics, I’m passionate about <strong>creative writing</strong>, <strong>poetry and declamation</strong>, and recreational <strong>jogging</strong>. I also have a deep interest in <strong>history</strong> and <strong>philosophy</strong>—topics I often explore in conversation and writing. To combine these interests with my mathematical background, I founded and currently organize the <strong>Math History Seminar</strong> at Purdue.
</p>

<p>
I completed my undergraduate studies at <strong>Rutgers University</strong>, where I majored in <strong>mathematics</strong> and <strong>philosophy</strong>. Later, I earned an <strong>M.S. in Applied Mathematics</strong> at the <strong>New Jersey Institute of Technology</strong>, where I worked with <strong>Dr. Christina Frederick</strong> on forward and inverse scattering problems in the field of <strong>underwater acoustics</strong>.
</p>
